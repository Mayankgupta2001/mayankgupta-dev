STEMSaathi, from first visit to paid order to classroom

The biggest thing I've shipped. I own the whole web platform.

STEMSaathi supplies robotics and electronics kits to schools. The website had to feel trustworthy to parents and school staff, take real online payments, and later deliver courses to students.

What I built

  • E-shop with 164 products, category pages, guest checkout and GST-inclusive prices
  • ICICI Eazypay integration: request hashing, callbacks, automatic order confirmation, success and failure pages
  • Admin panel for products, orders and leads, with bulk product import
  • Backend on Next.js, Prisma and PostgreSQL, with email notifications and spam protection
  • LMS for schools: admin-created accounts, assigned courses, video lessons, lesson completion tracking
  • SEO setup: Search Console, clean URLs, on-page and content plan
Visit stemsaathi.com

Problems I had to solve

The payment application was rejected

ICICI turned down our first application because the site showed B2B and course content. I restructured the site as a B2C shop, resubmitted, and got approved.

Checkout failed domain validation

ICICI's logs showed no Referer header reaching them. My redirect used rel=noreferrer, which strips it. A standard redirect fixed it and live payments started working.

100+ products imported with wrong images

Image paths didn't match product names. I built a category-to-folder map with fuzzy filename matching, so every product loads with the right photo.
